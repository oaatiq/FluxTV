# MotionTV privacy policy

_Effective 27 November 2026._

MotionTV is an app for watching TV channels from a playlist you add yourself, on Android TVs, TV
boxes, phones and tablets. It is made by **Oussama AATIQ** ("we"). This policy says what
MotionTV does with information about you. In short: **we collect nothing**. MotionTV has no account,
no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it never sends anything to us.

## What stays on your device

MotionTV keeps these on your device, in its own private storage, so that it can work:

- the playlists you add (their names and addresses, which may include the user name and password
  your TV provider put in them), and the channels, favorites and last channel of each;
- the programme guide it downloads for those channels;
- your settings, including the optional Settings PIN;
- a diagnostics history (when channels started, stopped or recovered, and why), which never holds
  playlist addresses.

None of this leaves your device, except as described below. Uninstalling MotionTV, or clearing its
storage in Android's settings, deletes all of it.

If you have turned on Android's backup to your Google account, Android may include MotionTV's
settings and playlists in that backup, so they can come back on a new device. That backup is
handled by Google under your Google account, not by us, and we cannot see it.

## What MotionTV connects to

MotionTV connects only to the addresses you give it, and only to do what you asked:

- **Your TV provider.** MotionTV downloads the playlist, the channels' video and sound, the
  programme guide and the channel logos from the addresses in the playlist you added. Like any app or
  browser that opens an address, this tells those servers your device's IP address and the name
  MotionTV gives itself (or the one you set in Settings → Preferences). What your provider does with
  that is covered by your provider's own terms and privacy policy, not by this policy.
- **The demo playlist**, if you choose "Use sample test playlist": it is downloaded from GitHub, and
  its films and test streams from the servers that publish them (Unified Streaming, the DASH Industry
  Forum, Mux and Akamai).
- **Adding a playlist from your phone.** While the "Add channels using your phone" screen is open,
  MotionTV answers on your home network at an address shown on the TV, protected by a random code
  that only the QR code holds. The link you send from your phone goes straight to the TV over your
  home network. It closes when you leave that screen, or after 15 minutes.

MotionTV does not sell, share or transfer any information to us or to anyone else, and it has no
advertising or analytics libraries.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state**: to download playlists, the guide and the channels, and to know
  when the network is back after a drop.
- **Start at boot** and **Display over other apps** (Android 10 and newer, only if you allow it):
  so that MotionTV can open itself, on the last channel, when the TV is turned on. MotionTV never
  draws over other apps.
- **Foreground service (media playback)**: on a phone or tablet, only if you turn on "Keep playing
  with the screen off", so that a channel's sound continues while the screen is off.
- **Wake lock**: to keep the device awake while a channel plays.

## Children

MotionTV is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone,
children included.
